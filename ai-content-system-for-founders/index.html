Why another prompt rarely fixes generic output

A model can imitate a tone from instructions. It cannot know which client story is real, which industry belief you reject, which result you can prove, or which recommendation cost you something unless that material is supplied.

That is why “write conversationally” produces cleaner sentences but not stronger authorship. The missing layer is source context: the founder's business, buyers, beliefs, examples, language and anti-language.
