QuestionsThe real ones
Which AI do I need?

Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini — any current paid tier. The Engine is deliberately model-agnostic. Models change every quarter; mechanisms don't.

Is this just prompts?

No. Prompts are one of ten components, and they're the least important one. The Source File, the Idea Quality Score and the Human Editor System are what make the prompts work at all.

Will my content sound like AI?

Not if you run the system. The Human Editor module exists specifically to prevent it — a De-Slop pass, a tell-list, specificity injection and a 7-point publish gate. And your Source File gets more accurate every month, because you feed corrections back into it.

I already use AI for content. What does this add?

Context and judgment. Most people run excellent prompts against terrible context, then edit for polish instead of specificity. This fixes the order.

How long before it works?

You'll have a publishable asset within an hour and a working rhythm within a month. Business results from content are a lagging indicator — anyone who tells you otherwise is selling something worse than this.

Do I need design skills?

No. The product teaches words first, design second, deliberately. A well-argued carousel in plain type beats a beautiful one with nothing to say.

Is my data private?

Completely. There's no server and no account. Everything you type stays in your browser and works offline. There's a one-click backup file if you want to move machines.

What if I have no proof or case studies yet?

The Proof Inventory prompt tells you exactly what to start documenting so that in 90 days you have evidence you currently lack. The Engine will never invent it for you — that's the entire point.
